Sponsorship Activities

2022 1st Dreaming Youth Contest

I organized this contest to support teenagers outside the Seoul metropolitan area.
Initially, I planned to fund it with approximately KRW 2 million of my own money, including KRW 1.2 million in prizes and operating expenses. With later support from designer Gyumin Jung, Saengneung Publishing, and Geekble Inc., the total prize pool reached KRW 3.3 million.
We selected 18 students, awarded prize money and plaques, and published With Dreams That Shine Like Stars, a book sharing their stories. All book sales proceeds are donated to the Korea Childhood Cancer Foundation.

Church Brothers Busking Team: Benefit Performances for Children with Cancer

A regular busking series that began in November 2019, with performances every Tuesday and Thursday. Unfortunately, the worsening COVID-19 pandemic brought the project to a halt before spring 2020. We raised approximately KRW 900,000 during the project and donated all proceeds to the Korea Childhood Cancer Foundation.
