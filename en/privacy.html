Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

NaNa Lab, represented by Byunghyun Ban, processes user information as follows when handling inquiries and operating bhban.kr.

1. Information Entered When Making an Inquiry

The inquiry form collects your name, organization, email address, phone number, inquiry subject, and message. Required and optional fields follow the indications on each form. This information is used to answer inquiries, discuss lectures and collaboration, and maintain related contact. Optional fields may be left blank. If information needed for a response is missing, we may be unable to accept or answer the inquiry.

2. Transmission and Retention of Inquiry Information

Information entered in the inquiry form is sent to the operator's email address through EmailJS. EmailJS and the connected email service process this information during transmission, and EmailJS may retain transmission requests and processing records. EmailJS's official privacy policy states that it uses servers located in the United States.

The operator retains inquiry emails for up to one year from receipt and deletes them earlier when they are no longer needed. Information subject to a separate statutory retention obligation is retained for the period required by the applicable law. Processing records held by external services such as EmailJS are governed by each service's retention policies and settings.

EmailJS Privacy Policy

3. Visitor Statistics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand visitor statistics and site usage. Through cookies and similar technologies, Google may process information about pages visited, access times, devices and browsers, and activity on the site.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Analytics Opt-Out Tool

4. Advertising and Cookies

When Google AdSense ads are served on this site, Google and third-party advertising providers may use cookies to show ads based on visits to this or other sites. Google advertising cookies may be used to deliver ads and measure performance.

You can manage personalized ads in Google's ad settings and opt out of personalized advertising from participating providers through the industry advertising choice tool. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. External Services and Content

This site uses external services, including GitHub Pages for web hosting, YouTube videos, and Google Fonts. When pages and external content load, providers may process information needed to deliver their services, such as IP addresses, browser information, and access logs. After you follow a link to an external site, its privacy policy governs information processing there.

GitHub Privacy Statement · Google and YouTube Privacy Policy

6. User Requests and Inquiries

To request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of information you have provided to the operator, please use the contact page. We may request identity verification to the extent necessary to confirm your request. If applicable law requires retention, we will explain the reason. The contact person for privacy inquiries is Byunghyun Ban, head of NaNa Lab.

7. Changes to This Policy

If changes in operations or services require this policy to be revised, the changes and effective date will be posted on this page.
